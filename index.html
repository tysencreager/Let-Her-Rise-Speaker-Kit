05 · DM & Text Scripts

For your warm network.

Highest converting channel for events like this. Use these for 1:1 outreach to your closest circle — friends, peers, past clients you genuinely think would benefit.

The 90-Second Rule

Every templated DM needs ONE specific line added before you send it. "Thought of you because of [the season you're in / your launch / our last conversation]." That one line is the difference between an outreach that converts and one that gets ignored. Don't skip it.

1. Personal Invite

For your close network — friends, peers, past clients.

Script A — Warm and direct

Hey [NAME] — I'm speaking at a women's leadership conference in Utah this August (Let Her Rise, Aug 26–28) and I genuinely thought of you when I was looking at the invite list I want to bring. [ONE SPECIFIC LINE — why this one is for them] Want me to send you the link? No pressure, just thought you should know it exists.

Script B — Casual (closer friends)

Quick one — are you doing anything Aug 26–28? I'm speaking at a women's biz conference here in Utah and the more I think about it the more I think you'd love it. Want details or want me to leave you alone? 😄

Script C — For past clients

Hey [NAME], hope [SPECIFIC THING — their business / family / project] is going well. I'm speaking at Let Her Rise this August — it's a 3-day women's leadership conference in Lindon, and I keep thinking about how much of what I'm sharing on stage maps to stuff we worked through together. Felt weird not to tell you about it. Want the link? Early Bird is still open.

2. "Thinking of You" Follow-Up

Send 5–7 days after the first invite — only if you'd genuinely follow up.

Soft re-engage

Hey [NAME] — circling back on the conference thing. No pressure at all. Just wanted to make sure I didn't let it slip past you in case you were on the fence. [YOUR LINK]

Add a value pull

Hey [NAME] — I know I sent you the Let Her Rise info a bit ago. Not following up to pressure you, but I just got the speaker list updated and [SPECIFIC SPEAKER OR DETAIL — e.g., "Jeff Griffin, USA Paralympian, is doing the keynote and his story is unreal"]. Thought you'd find that interesting. Anyway — here if you want it: [LINK]

3. Group Chat / Community Blast

Slack, Discord, group thread, mastermind — where everyone knows you.

Group chat / text thread

Posting once and never again, promise 😄 I'm speaking at Let Her Rise Aug 26–28 in Lindon — 3-day women's leadership conference, 8 speakers, faith-centered, built for the woman who's doing the real work behind the scenes. If anyone wants the deets, here's the link: [YOUR LINK] Early Bird is open + I'd genuinely love to see any of you there.

4. Last-Chance Nudge

For specific people who showed interest but didn't pull the trigger.

The honest reminder

Hey [NAME] — quick heads up that Early Bird for Let Her Rise closes [DATE]. After that it's $100 more per seat. Wanted to make sure you knew before the window closed. If it's a no, totally cool. If it's a maybe, here's the link: [YOUR LINK]

The personal pull

Hey [NAME] — I'm not gonna keep bugging you about this, but Early Bird for Let Her Rise closes [DATE] and I keep thinking I'd really love to see you in that room. Last gentle nudge. [YOUR LINK]

5. Response Handlers

When people push back. Drop these into the thread when needed.

"It's too expensive."

Totally get it — and to be fair, it's $297 for 3 full days, 8 speakers, and a sound bath if you go VIP. That's about $100/day for a conference of this caliber. But I get if the timing's off — no pressure either way. Just wanted to make sure you had the full picture.

"I can't take 3 days off."

Yeah, I hear that a lot. Quick thought though — the agenda runs 9–6 daily, which means you could honestly come for whichever day fits your schedule and still get a ton of value. Day 1 is "Recognize," Day 2 is "Rewire," Day 3 is "Rise" — each builds on the last but each stands alone too. Worth at least looking at the schedule.

"Utah's too far."

Fair. It IS Utah. But I'll tell you what I've heard from women who've traveled in for events like this: the right room is worth a flight. Salt Lake's a quick connect from most major cities and Lindon's 40 mins from the airport. If you're curious, I can send you the practical details.

"I'm not sure it's for me / not sure I'd fit in."

I think everyone in that room is going to be feeling that exact thing on Day 1, so you'd be in good company 😄. Honestly though — if you're a woman building something (a business, a brand, a life on your terms) and you're tired of doing it alone, this is exactly the room. Want me to send you the speaker bios so you can see if it lands?

"I'll think about it." / "Maybe next year."

Sounds good — and just so you have it: Early Bird closes [DATE], $100 jump after that. If you want a reminder before the window closes I'm happy to send one.
06 · Talking Points

For when your mouth opens.

Chamber meetings, masterminds, podcasts, coffee chats. Memorize the version that fits the moment.

15-Second Mention

Drop-in moments, "what's new with you?", quick networking exchanges.

I'm actually speaking at a women's leadership conference in Utah this August — Let Her Rise. Three days, eight women, faith-centered. It's for women entrepreneurs who are tired of pushing through and ready to build something more sustainable. Aug 26–28.

30-Second Elevator Pitch

For networking events, masterminds, "tell me more."

I'm speaking at Let Her Rise — it's a three-day conference for women entrepreneurs happening Aug 26–28 in Lindon, Utah. Eight speakers, faith-centered, and the audience is specifically women who are doing the real work of building businesses while navigating real life — burnout, chronic illness, motherhood, ADHD, perimenopause, the actual stuff. I'm bringing my talk on [YOUR TOPIC] because [ONE-SENTENCE WHY YOU CARE]. Early Bird is open if you know someone who'd love it — or want to come yourself.

Podcast intros, "tell me your whole story," 1:1 meetings.

Let me tell you about something I'm really excited about. I'm one of eight speakers at Let Her Rise this August — a women's leadership conference in Utah hosted by Jeana Nunez. What makes it different from every other women's event I've been to is the audience: it's specifically built for women entrepreneurs who are doing the real work behind the scenes. Not the highlight reel. [INSERT YOUR PERSONAL HOOK — e.g., "When I was building my first business, I was simultaneously navigating [SPECIFIC CHALLENGE] and there was no room I could walk into where I didn't have to either pretend everything was fine or unpack the whole thing. Let Her Rise is built to be that missing room."] My talk is on [TALK TITLE] — and the heart of it is [ONE-SENTENCE PROMISE]. Aug 26–28, Lindon, UT. If anyone you know would feel seen in that room, send them my way.

The "Hard Ask" Script

For one specific person you really want in the room. Use sparingly — its power is in its specificity.

Can I tell you something a little forward? Of everyone I've talked to about Let Her Rise, you are at the top of the list of people I'd love to have in that room. [SPECIFIC REASON — e.g., 'You're navigating the exact season Jeana built this for' / 'Your work and my talk are saying parallel things' / 'You've told me twice this year how alone this all feels — this is the room that fixes that.'] I'm not going to push you. But I want you to know that if you came, you'd have someone in the room who already sees you and already wants you there. That's me. Early Bird closes [DATE]. Will you at least look at the link?

Common Questions People Will Ask

Have these ready — they come up every conversation.

"Where is it?"

Castle Park Event Center in Lindon, Utah — about 40 minutes south of the Salt Lake City airport.

"Who's it for?"

Women entrepreneurs and business owners — specifically the ones who are tired of pushing through. There's a real focus on doing this work while navigating real life: chronic illness, perimenopause, ADHD, faith, motherhood. It's for anyone building something on her own terms.

"Is it religious?"

It's faith-centered, but not preachy. Jeana built it as a God-centered space for women entrepreneurs — meaning faith is the foundation, not the marketing angle. You don't need to belong to a specific tradition to feel welcome.

"How much is it?"

Early Bird is $297 for Standard, $447 for VIP. Goes up by $100 after Early Bird closes. VIP is capped at 50 and includes extra workshops, a sound bath, and morning experiences.

"Who else is speaking?"

Eight women across faith, business, body, and leadership. Plus a keynote from Jeff Griffin — he's a USA Paralympian whose story is genuinely one of the most powerful I've heard.
