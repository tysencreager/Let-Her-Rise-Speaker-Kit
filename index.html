05 · DM & Text Scripts

For your warm network.

This is where most of your tickets will actually come from. Use these for 1:1 outreach to your closest circle: friends, peers, past clients who you actually think would love it.

One Quick Rule

Every templated DM needs ONE specific line added before you send it. "Thought of you because of [whatever you actually thought of, their launch, the season they're in, your last conversation]." That single line is the difference between something that lands and something that gets ignored. Don't skip it.

1. Personal Invite

For your close network: friends, peers, past clients.

Warm and direct
Hey [NAME], I'm speaking at a women's leadership conference here in Utah this August (Let Her Rise, Aug 26 to 28) and I thought of you when I was making my invite list. [ONE SPECIFIC LINE: why this one is for them] Want me to send you the link? No pressure either way, just thought you should know about it.
Casual (closer friends)
Quick one, are you doing anything Aug 26 to 28? I'm speaking at a women's business conference here in Utah and the more I think about it the more I think you'd love it. Want the details, or want me to leave you alone? 😄

Send 5 to 7 days after the first invite, only if you'd actually follow up.

5. When People Push Back

Quick responses for the most common pushback.

"It's too expensive."
Totally get it. For what it's worth, $297 for 3 full days and 8 speakers works out to about $100 a day, which is on the lower end for a conference like this. But I get if the timing's just off, no pressure. Just wanted to make sure you had the full picture.
"I can't take 3 days off."
Yeah, I hear that a lot. Quick thought though, the agenda runs 9 to 6 daily, so you could come for whichever day fits your schedule and still get a ton out of it. Day 1 is "Recognize," Day 2 is "Rewire," Day 3 is "Rise." Each builds on the last, but each stands alone too. Worth at least looking at the schedule.
"I'll think about it." / "Maybe next year."
Sounds good. Just so you have it: Early Bird closes [DATE] and it's a $100 jump after that. If you want a reminder before the window closes, happy to send one.

Using Gmail, Outlook, or Apple Mail? Use "Copy for Gmail". That copies the rendered signature (with formatting), which is the only thing those signature editors accept.

How to add it to your email

Gmail (desktop)
  1. Click "Copy for Gmail" above on the version you want.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon (top right) → See all settings.
  3. Scroll to the Signature section. Click "Create new" if you don't already have one, or click the pencil icon next to your existing signature to edit it.
  4. Place your cursor where you want the Let Her Rise block to appear (typically a line or two below your normal signature) and paste (Cmd+V on Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
  5. Scroll to the bottom of settings and click Save Changes. Send yourself a test email to confirm it looks right.

Common gotcha: if you paste and it shows as raw code (the literal <table> text), you copied the HTML instead. Hit "Copy for Gmail" instead of "Copy HTML" and try again.

Outlook (desktop & web)
  1. Click "Copy for Gmail" (the button works for Outlook too).
  2. Outlook web: Gear icon → View all Outlook settings → Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature.
  3. Outlook desktop: File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  4. Paste the signature into the editor box.
  5. Save. Send yourself a test.
Apple Mail (Mac)
  1. Click "Copy for Gmail".
  2. Mail → Settings (or Preferences) → Signatures.
  3. Pick the account, click + to create a new signature.
  4. Paste into the signature box on the right.
  5. Make sure "Always match my default message font" is unchecked, otherwise Apple Mail will strip the styling.
  6. Close the window. Compose a new email to confirm it appears.
MailerLite / Flodesk / ConvertKit / Mailchimp

These platforms accept raw HTML, so use the "Copy HTML" button instead. Paste the HTML into the signature editor in source/HTML mode.

MailerLite: Account settings → Email signature → switch to HTML view.
Flodesk: Workflow / template editor → Edit code block.
Most platforms have an HTML toggle in their signature editor.

After Aug 28

Remove this signature once the event is over (or replace with a "Just spoke at Let Her Rise, replay coming" version). Don't leave a dated event promo running indefinitely.
